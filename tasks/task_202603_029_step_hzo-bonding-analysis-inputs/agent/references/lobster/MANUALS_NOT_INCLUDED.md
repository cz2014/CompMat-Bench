# LOBSTER manuals not included

The agent was given the LOBSTER 5.1.1 User's Guide and FAQ (PDF and
`pdftotext -layout` extracts) in this directory. They are not redistributed:
the LOBSTER download agreement states that "the software or any part thereof
shall not be made accessible to third parties without the explicit written
consent of SSC/RWTH" (Chair of Solid-State and Quantum Chemistry, RWTH Aachen
University), and the manuals are distributed only inside that download.

To restore the directory, download LOBSTER 5.1.1 from http://www.cohp.de/
(download page https://schmeling.ac.rwth-aachen.de/cohp/index.php?menuID=6;
registration and acceptance of the licence required), copy the two PDFs from
the package here and write the text extracts with `pdftotext -layout`. The
PDFs should match the checksums below; the extracts may differ by pdftotext
version. No grader reads these files.
